To restore the archive, place the recording, captions, and thumbnail in this directory. Calculate each checksum with `sha256sum <filename>` and copy `manifest.example.json` to `manifest.json`, recording the retrieval date, source URL, filenames, and checksums. The media and completed manifest remain ignored by Git.
